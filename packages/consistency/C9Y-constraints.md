# C9Y constraints

The rules the Consistency editor keeps, written from `src/c9y-constraints.ts` by `vp run c9y-constraints`. Do not edit this file by hand.

Each rule names the test that holds it. While editing a site, a drag that breaks `drag.one-movement` outlines the blocks that moved again and says so in the editor's notice.

## Rules

### `controls.edit-position`

The Edit action stays centred above its control, including after resizing, while pressing the control itself follows its normal action.

Held by `tests/control-edit.mjs`: “Hover Edit works for native links, imported links and DS1 buttons”.

### `spacing.always-visible`

Every editable spacer stays pink while idle, with a visible centre line at zero, across all example pages.

Held by `tests/template-spacing.mjs`: “idle pink guides”.

### `spacing.vertical-growth`

Content containers grow vertically when edited spacing needs more room, including after resizing and reopening, instead of clipping their words.

Held by `tests/template-spacing.mjs`: “resized page grows with its spacing after reopening”.

### `navigation.no-nesting`

Following a canvas link never loads an editor inside the editor; local anchors remain in the page and external destinations open separately.

Held by `tests/template-spacing.mjs`: “safe links”.

### `canvas.component-assets`

Site-owned device artwork and chrome load through the authenticated file gateway in inline and URL canvases, including after reopening.

Held by `tests/carousel-frames.mjs`: “Device frames load through site files and carousel insertion stays unavailable”.

### `carousel.existing-content`

Carousel slides keep their existing editable words, while menus and insertion commands cannot add writing, groups, sections or generated blocks inside them.

Held by `tests/carousel-frames.mjs`: “Device frames load through site files and carousel insertion stays unavailable”.

### `carousel.imported-row`

Imported carousel tracks keep each authored slide whole and only reorder horizontally, both while carrying and after release.

Held by `tests/carousel-imported.mjs`: “Imported showcase cards stay in one horizontal row”.

### `emails.templates`

Editable email wording saves automatically with revision checks, keeps newer typing and failed drafts, previews real form placeholders and survives reopening without sending an email.

Held by `tests/places-shared.mjs`: “Enquiry templates save automatically, preserve newer typing and failed drafts, preview fields and reopen”.

### `carousel.reorder`

Dragging a carousel card by its body or picture reorders the complete card only within its own horizontal row, never moves it vertically or nests it, preserves its contents and saves the order for reopening and publication.

Held by `tests/carousel-reorder.mjs`: “Carousel cards drag horizontally, save and reopen in their new order”.

### `selection.writing-only`

Browser text selection stays inside the active text editor or input; dragging over the canvas and Select All outside writing never select page text.

Held by `tests/text-selection.mjs`: “Canvas selection stays within active writing”.

### `drag.appearance`

Lifting and carrying an element preserves its appearance, including typography, colours, dimensions and text wrapping; only its position changes.

Held by `tests/drag-appearance.mjs`: “Dragged content preserves typography, wrapping and appearance”.

### `native.templates`

Every authored template retains its words and desktop/phone layout through the shared block renderer.

Held by `tests/template-native.mjs`: “content and design preserved through shared blocks”.

### `native.inline-writing`

Buttons and links keep their normal press; their hover Edit action edits words in place with spaces, selection, saving and reopening, without a page-wide editing mode.

Held by `tests/control-edit.mjs`: “Hover Edit works for native links, imported links and DS1 buttons”.

### `native.workspace`

All lab pages, templates and private site drafts use the published page model and editor; spacing survives saving and reopening, and a refused save retains the local document for recovery.

Held by `tests/workspace-native.mjs`: “Shared workspace editor: native blocks”.

### `spacing.hit-area`

In Chromium and WebKit, resize labels never intercept a neighbouring spacing handle; pointer and keyboard spacing changes persist after reopening.

Held by `tests/soendergaarden-spacing.mjs`: “Søndergården front: publication retry”.

### `spacing.sticky-header`

Scrolling a sticky header over the page never creates a spacing handle across content; paragraph gaps remain directly draggable.

Held by `tests/soendergaarden-spacing.mjs`: “Scrolled paragraph gaps stay directly draggable”.

### `native.gutters`

Native gallery guides cover only the real whitespace between overlapping tracks or complete rows, including after scrolling; they never cross product content.

Held by `src/native-page-model.test.ts`: “maps two featured and three ordinary gallery items to actual whitespace”.

### `native.publication`

A native page edits the same typed blocks and responsive measures that publication renders, with each block owning its mobile defaults and every saved gesture surviving reopening.

Held by `tests/native-page.mjs`: “Native page: real model/scroll/public geometry”.

### `native.sections`

A section of the vocabulary is one block: selected, moved, hidden, removed and resized whole; its fields are set from its menu inside the vocabulary's ranges, its words edited in place in their slots, and its items reordered and removed inside it, never below the kind's minimum.

The renderer expands a section to primitives, so the editor reads the same expansion and never guesses a layout from the HTML; the vocabulary is the one record of what a field may be.

Held by `src/native-page-model.test.ts`: “edits a section as one block: slots, items and ranges from the vocabulary”.

### `drag.one-movement`

While a block is carried, the blocks around it move out of the way once for each change of place, straight to where the dropped order will draw them, and nothing moves again on release.

Someone dragging is rearranging the layout; a page that lands and then corrects itself has guessed wrong in front of them. Drag measures each list's flow at lift (sizes, margins, gap, collapsing margins) so the room it makes is the layout the drop commits.

Held by `src/c9y-constraints.test.ts`: “siblings move once, straight to the committed layout, for every move in a list”.

### `drag.only-while-carried`

Blocks move out of the way only while a block is carried: never before it lifts, and never after the drop has settled.

Held by `src/c9y-constraints.test.ts`: “the ledger reports any movement after release as a broken constraint”.

### `drag.change-of-place`

A block makes room once the carried block's edge passes its centre, and gives it back only when the edge passes back, so a pointer resting near a centre never makes blocks flicker.

Held by `src/c9y-constraints.test.ts`: “a sibling makes room past its centre and does not flicker back”.

### `drag.single-landing`

A dropped block lands once, directly at its committed place, within a pixel; the editor commits the final layout before the carried block lands.

Held by `tests/reorder.mjs`: “Single landing”.

### `drag.grid`

Where blocks and the spaces between them are whole squares, every move out of the way is a whole number of squares.

Design system 1 draws everything on the 20px square multiplied by --sf; a block that makes room never leaves the grid, even for a frame.

Held by `src/c9y-constraints.test.ts`: “room made on the grid is whole squares”.

### `spacing.whole-squares`

Every space, padding, width and height the editor writes is a whole number of 20px squares, with widths of at least ten squares and heights of at least four.

Held by `src/c9y-constraints.test.ts`: “measures the editor writes stay on whole squares”.

### `spacing.owned-by-block`

The space above or below a block belongs to that block and travels with it when it moves, never to the position it stands in.

Space that depends on position (the last block losing its gap) changes the layout after the drop, which breaks drag.one-movement.

Held by `src/c9y-constraints.test.ts`: “a block carries its own space when it moves”.

### `blocks.capabilities`

Each kind of block offers exactly the handles its row in the block table gives it; the editor never draws a handle the platform would refuse.

Held by `src/c9y-constraints.test.ts`: “every block kind has its rules and capabilities”.

### `blocks.reorder`

A block can be moved among its siblings only when it can be placed and has at least one sibling.

Held by `src/c9y-constraints.test.ts`: “a block reorders only among siblings”.

### `blocks.protected`

A protected region and the page root can be measured but never hidden, placed or moved.

Held by `src/c9y-constraints.test.ts`: “protected regions and the root never move”.

### `motion.transform-only`

Dragging animates translate, transform and opacity only, never a property that changes layout.

Held by `src/c9y-constraints.test.ts`: “drag motion animates translate, transform and opacity only”.

### `motion.reduced`

With reduced motion, a release applies the final layout at once, with the same geometry and no animation.

Held by `src/settle.test.ts`: “release uses translation only and reduced motion applies layout immediately”.

## Blocks

Capabilities come from the platform; `reorder` is added where `blocks.reorder` allows it. Measures are whole 20px squares multiplied by `--sf`.

### root

The page itself.

- Capabilities: `width`, `padding`, `gap`, `insert`
- Children make room: down the page
- Never hidden, placed or moved; its width, padding and gap are the page's.
- New blocks can be inserted into it.

### container

A section of the site's own markup that holds blocks.

- Capabilities: `width`, `marginTop`, `marginBottom`, `hide`, `place`, `padding`, `gap`, `insert`
- Children make room: down the page
- Its children make room down the page.
- Its padding and gap are whole squares; blocks can be inserted into it.

### group

A flow of blocks the editor made by grouping.

- Capabilities: `width`, `marginTop`, `marginBottom`, `hide`, `place`, `padding`, `gap`, `insert`
- Children make room: down the page
- Behaves as a container: its children make room down the page, and blocks can be inserted into it.

### columns

Blocks placed side by side.

- Capabilities: `width`, `marginTop`, `marginBottom`, `hide`, `place`, `padding`, `gap`, `insert`
- Children make room: across
- Its columns make room across, not down.
- Placing a block beside another makes a columns block; its gap is the space between columns.

### column

One column of a columns block.

- Capabilities: `width`, `marginTop`, `marginBottom`, `hide`, `place`, `padding`, `gap`, `insert`
- Children make room: down the page
- A column with a single block moves with that block when the block is carried out.

### text

Words: a paragraph, heading or link drawn by Aa.

- Capabilities: `width`, `marginTop`, `marginBottom`, `hide`, `place`
- Children make room: it holds no blocks
- Its words are edited in place; a press on its words drags it only once it is selected.
- Its spacing above and below belongs to it.

### image

A picture.

- Capabilities: `width`, `marginTop`, `marginBottom`, `hide`, `place`
- Children make room: it holds no blocks
- Its width is whole squares; its height follows its picture.
- A selected image draws its selection over the picture, not around the frame.

### card

A DS1 card.

- Capabilities: `width`, `marginTop`, `marginBottom`, `hide`, `place`, `height`, `design`
- Children make room: it holds no blocks
- Width and height are whole squares, never below its minimum.
- Its tone and outline are design; its words stay its own when designs are shared.

### stat

A DS1 figure with its label and caption.

- Capabilities: `width`, `marginTop`, `marginBottom`, `hide`, `place`, `design`
- Children make room: it holds no blocks
- Its inner gaps are whole squares.
- Its design can be shared; its words cannot.

### button

A DS1 button or link styled as one.

- Capabilities: `width`, `marginTop`, `marginBottom`, `hide`, `place`, `design`
- Children make room: it holds no blocks
- Its action is a link or a page of the site.
- Its appearance is plain, primary or secondary.

### carousel

A row of slides that scrolls sideways.

- Capabilities: `width`, `marginTop`, `marginBottom`, `hide`, `place`, `height`
- Children make room: it holds no blocks
- The carousel moves as one block; its slides scroll in its own frame and never widen the page.
- Its height is whole squares.

### region

Native content the site renders: a menu, a form, a booking.

- Capabilities: `width`, `marginTop`, `marginBottom`, `hide`, `place`, `padding`
- Children make room: it holds no blocks
- Its contents belong to the site; only its width, padding and spacing are edited.
- Protected regions are never hidden or moved.
