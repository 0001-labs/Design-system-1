A panel is a column of chrome standing on the paper: a header pinned to its top, a body that scrolls on its own, and a footer pinned to its bottom. An application's list beside its detail, an inspector, a drawer's contents are panels.

## Use it

```html
<ds-panel>
  <ds-row slot="header" fill>
    <ds-text literal strong>Projects</ds-text>
    <ds-button primary>New</ds-button>
  </ds-row>
  <ds-list>…</ds-list>
  <ds-row slot="footer"><ds-text tone="muted" literal>12 projects</ds-text></ds-row>
</ds-panel>
```

Content goes to the default slot; `slot="header"` and `slot="footer"` pin to the top and bottom. Give the panel its height from the page's layout (a [page](/4-page/page/)'s columns, `fill`) and only the body scrolls. Rows inside it are one square high as everywhere; the panel adds no box or shadow of its own.

| Slot | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `header` | A row at the top: the panel's title and its actions. |
| Default | The body, which scrolls. |
| `footer` | A row at the bottom: a count, closing actions. |

| Part | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `header`, `body`, `footer` | The three areas, for a product's own styles. |

Select `panel` in Sync 1's components export.
