## What you are seeing

The overlay uses 19px tracks and 1px lines. It is fixed over the document and has `pointer-events: none`, so it does not intercept clicks or arrange children.

## Attribute

`align` accepts `left`, `center` or `right`. On a phone the overlay follows fourteen scaled columns. Keep the mobile track math intact.

## Page shortcuts

On the documentation, press **G three times** to toggle the shared page grid. Press **L three times** for labelled layout areas; give those areas `data-ds-area="name"`. Escape hides the overlays. Typing in a field does not trigger them.
