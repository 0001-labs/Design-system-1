There are two calendars, drawn from the same days and keys.

- **`ds-calendar`** draws a month the way a list draws its items: seven days across, a week to a row, each day a hairline box four squares wide and four high. Its date stands on the first square, two lines of events below it and one square to spare. With `compact`, a day is one square and the month becomes the small one, one of twelve in a year. With `picker`, it is a field that opens that small month to choose a date: see [the picker](#picker).
- **`ds-calendar-large`** draws months side by side as squares: a week to a column and the seven days down it, one and a half times the grid, in a rounded frame that scrolls sideways. A day on which something happens is filled with its colour, so a long piece of work reads as a band across the weeks.

## A month

```html
<ds-calendar month="2026-10" selectable week-numbers></ds-calendar>
```

```js
const calendar = document.querySelector("ds-calendar");
calendar.events = [
  { title: "Design review", start: "2026-10-03", time: "10:00", color: "var(--ds-zenith-blue)" },
  { title: "Launch", start: "2026-10-12", end: "2026-10-14", color: "var(--ds-apple-green)" },
];
calendar.addEventListener("change", event => console.log(event.detail.value));
```

| Measure | Size |
| --- | --- |
| A day | Four squares wide (`day-width`), its date's square, `lines` squares of events (two), and one square to spare. |
| The title and arrows | One square, one square above the days. The title is a button one control high with a chevron after it, filled by the pointer 4px past its words (see [months and years](#months-and-years)). Today and the arrows stand at the end: each arrow a chevron on a square of its own, one control high, in the title grey until the pointer fills it. |
| The weekday names | One square, in the title grey, at the start of each day. |
| A date | At the end of its first square, in the title grey, tabular figures. |
| Today | A disc nine tenths of a square across, in red for blood, the date in white. |
| The selected day | A square of the same size with the system's 4px corners, in Sharp blue, the date in white. |
| A range | Each end drawn as the selected day, the days between on a band of the blue at 16%. In a compact month the band is the marks' height on their line, from the middle of the first day to the middle of the last, so the ends and the band read as one bar. |
| An event | One square, drawn one pixel short, its words 4px from its edges; the time first, fainter. Filled with its `color` or the fill. |
| More events than lines | The last line says `+2`; pressing it lays the day's events over the days below. |
| A week number | `W42` in the compressed face, at the start of the week's first day, with `week-numbers`. |
| Lines | 1px in the line colour, centred on the grid lines. |

A day outside the month is an empty box. `fixed` always draws six weeks, so moving between months never changes the calendar's height.

### Compact

`compact` makes every day a square, one square across by default: the date centred in it, the weekday names a letter each, the month's name shortened, and no events written out. With `day-width="2"` each day is two squares each way, the weekday names take two letters, Today stands beside the arrows and the marks grow to a square and two thirds, for a month with more room. `outside-days` writes the days of the months before and after in the title grey; pressing one goes there. When the calendar has events, a day with one keeps the ink and a day without falls back to the title grey, so a year of small months shows where things happened.

### Heat

```html
<ds-calendar compact heat fixed></ds-calendar>
```

```js
calendar.events = [
  { title: "3 changes", start: "2026-07-08", count: 3 },
  { title: "12 changes", start: "2026-07-09", count: 12 },
];
```

`heat` lights each day by how much happens on it, as a contribution graph does. A day's amount is its events' `count`s added up (an event without one counts once, an event over several days counts on each). The busiest day of the month shown sets the scale, and every other day takes one of four steps of it: up to a quarter, a half, three quarters, or more. `heat-max` fixes the amount that lights a day fully, so months stay comparable. A day with nothing stays plain, and when the month has events at all its date falls back to the title grey; the neighbouring months' days are never lit.

| Step | Light paper | Dark paper | The date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The accent's pastel | Its dim | The ink; white on dark paper |
| 2 | Halfway to the accent | Halfway to the accent's dark version | The ink; white on dark paper |
| 3 | The accent (Apple green) | The accent's dark version | The ink |
| 4 | The accent deepened with 40% of the ink | The full accent | The ink |

The fill is the date's mark, the same size as today's disc and the selected square, with a picture's corners, so it stays inside the day's square and the grid; in a compact month the marks stand a pixel apart, as a contribution graph's squares do. Every step keeps its date at 4.5:1 or more. Today and the selection draw over the heat. The fills are the palette's tokens (`--one-apple-green-pastel`, `--one-apple-green`), each `light-dark()`; a page lights the month in another colour by setting `--ds-calendar-heat-1` … `-4` and their `--ds-calendar-heat-ink-1` … `-4` from the palette.

## Months in squares

```html
<ds-calendar-large start="2026-09" months="6"></ds-calendar-large>
```

| Measure | Size |
| --- | --- |
| A day | One square times `scale` (one and a half: 30px). |
| Lines | 1px times the scale, inside a frame whose corners are four tenths of a day. |
| Words | The heading size times the scale (19.5px). |
| The weekday names | Down the start, outside the scroll, every other one named; a letter each on a narrow screen. |
| The months | Each name over the week holding its first day; January and the first month carry the year. |
| A date | Written only on today, the selected day, and where an event starts or ends. |
| Today | A disc nine tenths of a day across in red for blood; the selected day a square of the ink. |
| A filled day | The event's `color`, Apple green by default; days two or more events share are split into equal bands. |

The title names the month at the start of the view as it scrolls. The arrows move a month at a time and Today scrolls to today's month; past the months drawn, `start` moves with them and `navigate` says so.

## Choosing a day or a range

```html
<ds-calendar compact selectable day-width="2" value="2026-10-14/2026-10-20"></ds-calendar>
```

There is one way to choose, in a month and in the picker: a person picks one day or a range in the same calendar, with nothing to switch. The first press chooses a day, and `value` is that day. The next press ends a range from it, before or after, and `value` becomes `start/end` (ISO 8601's interval); pressing the same day again keeps the one day. The press after that starts again. While the end is still to come, the day under the pointer previews it.

`change` fires with each press that chooses: `{ value, previous, start, end }`, `end` empty while it is still to come and the last day once it is chosen. A `value` set by the page is whole, so the next press starts again.

### Clear

A selectable calendar has **Clear** one square below its month, at the start: a secondary button one control high, in the shared word, disabled while nothing is chosen. It empties the value and fires `change` with `value` empty (`start` and `end` too). `clear()` does the same from code, in a month and in a picker (where it also closes the panel). `required` leaves Clear out; the picker's month uses the picker's own Clear.

## Months and years

Pressing the title travels by months and years, as a date picker's title does. The days go up to the twelve months of their year (the title reads the year and the arrows step a year), the year goes up to twelve years (the title reads them, `2016 – 2027`, and the arrows step twelve). Choosing a year shows its months; choosing a month shows its days and fires `navigate`. Nothing is chosen on the way.

| Measure | Size |
| --- | --- |
| A month or a year | One square high and whole squares wide, three across a narrow month (two squares each) and four across a wider one, rows one square apart. |
| Its mark | Nine tenths of a square high with a picture's corners: the chosen month or year in Sharp blue, today's in the today red, the pointer's in the fill. |
| The area | The days' height, so nothing below moves between levels; the level a person moves to fades in (opacity and scale, none with reduced motion). |

The months and years are a grid with one Tab stop: the arrow keys move (past December is the next year's January), Enter or Space chooses, and Escape goes back down a level without closing a picker. Month names and years come from the current language through `format.ts`. `no-header` leaves the travel out with the title.

## Picker

```html
<form>
  <ds-calendar picker name="when" label="When" literal min="2026-10-01"></ds-calendar>
</form>
```

```js
picker.addEventListener("change", event => {
  console.log(event.detail.value); // "2026-10-21", "2026-10-21/2026-10-24", "2026-10-21T09:00/2026-10-21T10:00", or "" when cleared
});
```

With `picker`, the calendar is a field one control high, its value written in it and a chevron (`chevron-down`) at its end. A range is one picker and one value (Oct 21 – 24, 2026), never two pickers side by side for its ends. Pressing it opens a panel half a square below, on the menu's frame: the first and last day written out where they can be typed, the compact month one square a day, an **Include time** switch and **Clear**.

It chooses as a month does. Each opening starts a new choice: the first day pressed is the value; the second, before or after, ends a range and closes the panel. Pressing the same day twice, or leaving after the first press, keeps one day.

The value is ISO 8601: a day, a day with its time, or two of them joined by a slash, which is also what a form sends. A value with a time turns Include time on; `time` sets it from the page.

| Value | Face |
| --- | --- |
| `2026-10-21` | Oct 21, 2026 |
| `2026-10-21/2026-10-24` | Oct 21 – 24, 2026 |
| `2026-10-21T09:00/2026-10-21T10:00` | Oct 21, 2026, 9:00 – 10:00 AM |

The face writes the value in the current language, a range as the language writes one. `length` writes a day `short`, `medium` (the default) or `long`; before a day is chosen the face reads `placeholder`, the shared words "Choose a date" by default, in the title grey.

| Measure | Size |
| --- | --- |
| The field | A field's face: one control high at the foot of its square, ten squares wide (`--ds-calendar-picker-width`), the words 4px in, the `chevron-down` icon (16px) 2px from the end. |
| The days to type | A field one control high on its own square for the first day and one for the last; with times on, each day's time on the square below it. One square spare after them. |
| The month | One square a day, seven squares wide: the month with its chevrons, the weekdays in a letter, six weeks with the neighbouring months' days in the title grey. |
| The marks | The chosen days are squares with 4px corners in Sharp blue, the days between on a band of it, today a red disc. |
| Include time, Clear | One square each, the switch at the end, set off by one-square separators with a line through them. |
| The frame | A 1px line with half a square inside it, no shadow: eight squares wide. |
| Its place | Half a square below the field, or above it when there is more room there, kept on screen. |

Turning times on gives the first day 9:00 and the last 10:00, which the time fields change; with times on, the panel stays open after the second press so they can be set.

A typed day is read when the person presses Enter or leaves the field: `2026-10-21`, or a date the browser can read such as "Oct 21, 2026". Anything else puts the field back as it was.

`required` leaves Clear out and tells the form a value is missing. `min` and `max` limit the days. A form's reset puts back the value the field started with. `readDateValue`, `writeDateValue` and `parseTypedDay` are exported for a page that reads or writes the value itself.

## Events

Both calendars take the same events, as a property:

| Field | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `title` | What happens. |
| `start` | The first day, `YYYY-MM-DD`. |
| `end` | The last day, for something over several days. |
| `time` | When it starts, written before the title in a month. |
| `color` | A token or colour for its fill. Take it from the palette. |
| `count` | How much it counts for in `heat`; one when left out. |
| `id` | Anything the page needs back; the calendar leaves it alone. |

A day lists its events longest first, then by time.

## Days and weeks

A day is written `YYYY-MM-DD` and stays on its date in every time zone. The week starts where the language starts it (Monday in most of Europe, Sunday in the United States and Japan); `week-start` sets it, `0`–`6` from Sunday or a day's English name. Month and weekday names come from the current language.

`min` and `max` limit the days a person may choose: the days outside them are drawn faint and cannot be selected. `today` draws another day as today, for a preview or a test.

The day arithmetic the calendars use is `DS1/0-face/calendar.ts`, plain TypeScript without Lit: `monthWeeks`, `addDays`, `addMonths`, `firstWeekday`, `weekdayNames`, `isoWeek`, `eventsByDay` and the rest.

## Accessibility

- Each calendar is a grid named by its month (or `label`), with one Tab stop on a day: the one moved to, the selected day, today, or the first. A selectable calendar is `aria-multiselectable`, and every day of the range is `aria-selected`.
- The arrow keys move a day, or a week across the weeks; Home and End go to the week's first and last day; Page Up and Page Down move a month, with Shift a year. A month moves on when the focus leaves it, and right to left the arrows turn round.
- Enter or Space presses the day. Each day is labelled with its whole date and what happens on it, and today is `aria-current="date"`; a selectable calendar marks the selected day with `aria-selected`.
- The arrows are named "Previous month" and "Next month" ("Previous year", "Next year", "Earlier years" and "Later years" among the months and years), and the month's name is announced when it changes. The title button is named by the title and "Choose a month and year". All the words are the shared words, in every language.
- A picker's field is a button with `aria-haspopup="dialog"` and `aria-expanded`, named by `label` and the value. Enter, Space or the down arrow opens the panel with the focus on the first chosen day (or today); a click opens it and leaves the focus on the field. The typed fields and times are named by the label and which end they are; the switch is `role="switch"`. Escape, a press outside, or the focus leaving closes the panel, and the focus goes back to the field.

| `day-width` | number | `4` (`1` compact) | Squares per day. |

| `week-numbers` | boolean | `false` | The week's number on its first day. |

| `heat` | boolean | `false` | Light each day by how much happens on it. |
| `heat-max` | number | The busiest day shown | The amount that lights a day fully. |
| `no-header` | boolean | `false` | Leave out the title and arrows. |
| `no-today` | boolean | `false` | Leave out the Today button. |
| `min`, `max` | string | — | The days a person may choose. |

### Activity squares

Use `activity` on `ds-calendar-large` for a compact contribution-style grid.
It defaults to one grid square per day, separates the squares, and fills dates
with their event colours. Dates and event descriptions remain available to
assistive technology and in a tooltip. Use `no-header` for a fixed month.

```html
<ds-calendar-large activity no-header start="2026-10" months="1"></ds-calendar-large>
```

Set `events` as a property, as with the full calendar. Days without events stay
quiet; repeated events on one day do not increase the colour intensity.
